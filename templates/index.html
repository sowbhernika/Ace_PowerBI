Setup & Connection

Checking…
Open WhatsApp on your phone → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → scan this code.
It refreshes automatically and connects when scanned.

First-time setup

1
Login to Power BI — double-click login_powerbi.bat, sign in with MFA, then press Enter in the terminal.
2
Connect WhatsApp — make sure Docker is running, then click Link with QR above and scan from your phone. (If already linked before, just Connect WhatsApp.)
3
Add managers in the Managers tab with phone, company and department.
4
Pick reports, then Send Now — or set a Schedule.
